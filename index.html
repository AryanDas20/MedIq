About this project

Why MediMind exists

Most health apps forget you the moment you close them. MediMind's goal is a single continuous record — your allergies, your injuries, your past reports — that every future interaction, human or AI, is checked against. That one change removes the most common failure in digital health tools: generic advice that ignores what already happened to you.

This build is a fully interactive front-end demo. There's no real medical AI or hospital backend behind it — it runs entirely in your browser using rule-based logic and local storage — but every screen, flow and interaction is real, so you can click through the whole concept end to end.
